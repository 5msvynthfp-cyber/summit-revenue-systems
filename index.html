How it works

Three steps. Your team still talks to the customer and decides who gets a truck. We just make sure the lead does not go cold in the meantime.

1

The missed call gets a text

Someone calls your shop and nobody can pick up. Within 60 seconds they get a text from your company, not from us.

2

The job is qualified and routed

The text asks what they need. No heat, no AC, or a leak can be flagged to your on-call phone. Routine work gets a callback time. We do not pretend to be you, and we do not dispatch a truck.

3

You get a weekly report

Each week you receive a short report of the missed calls and forms it caught, what the customer needed, and what was booked.

Common questions

How do you get the missed calls?

Your phone company forwards unanswered calls to a number we set up for your shop. If that path ever fails, callers reach your normal voicemail. You can turn forwarding off at any time.

Is texting my callers legal?

We treat the first missed-call text as a reply to a call the customer already placed, not as a marketing message. The first text identifies your company and tells the person how to opt out. Follow-up texts, if any, stay about that same request. We do not send promotions, coupons, or review requests. Texts after a web form go out only when the form includes a clear SMS consent checkbox and disclosure. Each shop is registered for business texting under its own legal name before any messages are sent.

Carriers and regulators have not published a rule that names “missed call text back” as its own category of consent, so we keep the program narrow on purpose. This is a description of how we operate, not legal advice. A lawyer should review your specific use before you go live.

Who shows up as the sender?

The texts go out in your shop’s name. Callers should feel like they are hearing from you. Summit is your vendor in the background, not the name on the message.

Are the replies a fixed script, or made up on the fly?

No. The replies follow a fixed script we agree on with you. It does not invent answers, and it does not pretend to be a person in your shop.

How long does it take to go live?

Setup on our side is straightforward. Carrier approval for business texting often takes about a week, and it can take longer if the paperwork is sent back. We start that registration first and set expectations before you depend on the system.

What do you need from me?

Your legal business name, EIN (your federal tax ID), website, business hours, on-call cell, and a backup contact. Carriers also expect a privacy policy and texting terms on your site. We will help you with that language.

What about calls that come in at night?

The first reply to a missed call can go out right away, including at night, because the person just called you. Any later automatic follow-up waits until morning hours.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime.
