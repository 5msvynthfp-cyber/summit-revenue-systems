Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how Summit Revenue Systems (“Summit,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website, email us, text us, or use our missed-lead recovery service.

1. Who we are

Summit Revenue Systems is a missed-lead recovery and business automation service for HVAC shops, operated by Christopher Howard.

2. This website

This site is a static website. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, pixels, or other third-party tracking scripts on these pages. We do not collect information automatically from casual visitors beyond what your browser and our hosting provider may record in ordinary server logs (such as an IP address, browser type, and the page requested) if the host keeps them.

If you email us, we receive whatever you include in that message, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and the content of your question.

3. If you become a customer

If you hire us, we collect business account information needed to set up and run the service. That typically includes your legal business name, EIN (federal tax ID), business address, website, contacts, on-call and backup phone numbers, hours, service area, billing details, and the wording you approve for customer texts.

We use this information to register business texting in your shop’s name, configure call forwarding and scripts, send the weekly report, provide support, and bill you.

4. Information about your callers

When an HVAC shop uses our service, we process information about people who called the shop or submitted a form, such as their phone number and the content of texts or short replies. We do this as a service provider (a data processor) for that shop, and only to:

  • Send the missed-call or consented form follow-up text
  • Qualify the request with a short script
  • Alert the shop when a message looks like an emergency
  • Book a callback
  • Prepare the shop’s weekly report
  • Honor opt-out requests and keep required records

We do not use caller information to market Summit’s services. We do not combine or share caller information across different shops.

5. Mobile numbers, SMS, and opt-in data

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in records, or consent data with third parties for their marketing. We use texting and voice providers only to deliver the messages and calls the service requires, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing that service.

Messages from Summit Revenue Systems

If you provide your mobile number and consent to receive texts from us (for example, when you request a demo, during onboarding, or for support), you agree to receive customer care and account messages from Summit Revenue Systems about missed-lead recovery setup, support, billing, and service updates.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out. After you opt out, we will send a one-time confirmation and then stop texting that number for this program.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email summitrevenuesystems@gmail.com.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.

You may also opt out by any reasonable means, including an email or written request to the address above. We honor opt-out requests promptly, and in all cases within 10 business days.

Messages from an HVAC shop that uses this service

Texts to a shop’s callers are sent in the shop’s name. Summit processes those messages only as the shop’s vendor. The usual way a caller opts in is by calling the shop (a consumer-initiated contact, which may receive a single informational reply about that request, plus limited follow-up about the same request) or by checking a clearly labeled SMS consent box on the shop’s web form, which includes these disclosures.

  • You may receive 1 to 3 informational messages about a single service request. Message frequency varies.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
  • These messages are not marketing and are not a condition of receiving service.

The shop should not share a caller’s mobile number or opt-in information with third parties for their marketing. Marketing texts, coupons, tune-up specials, and review requests are not part of this service.

6. How long we keep information

  • Email you send us: as long as needed to respond and to keep an ordinary business record.
  • Customer account and billing information: for the life of the account, and longer when tax or legal rules require it.
  • Caller numbers and message transcripts: 12 months, then deleted, unless the shop asks sooner for an export or deletion, or we must keep something longer to meet a legal requirement or to honor an opt-out list.

7. Service providers

We may use vendors for website hosting, email, texting, voice, and payment processing. Those vendors may process information only to provide their service to us or to the shop, not for their own marketing.

8. We do not sell personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold, subject to the shop’s instructions when we hold caller data as their processor, and subject to legal recordkeeping rules. Email summitrevenuesystems@gmail.com or write to the mailing address above. For texts, reply STOP at any time.

10. Children

This website and our service are for businesses, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the date above. If a change is material, we will also try to notify current customers by email.
