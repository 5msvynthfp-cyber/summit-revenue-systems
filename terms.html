1. The service

Summit provides missed-lead recovery for local businesses. When a call to your business is missed, a text can go out in your company name within 60 seconds. The script qualifies the caller, can flag emergencies to your cell, can book a callback, and we send you a weekly report of what it caught. We may also follow up on a web form when that form includes a clear SMS consent checkbox and disclosure.

We act as your vendor. When texts go to your callers, your business is the sender. You remain responsible for your customer relationships and for deciding how to handle each request.
